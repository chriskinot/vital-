Our Story

We built VITAL out of necessity.

VITAL is a product-led, direct-to-consumer health and wellness brand operating under MEDIKEA AFYA APP LTD. We started with a mission to make high quality personal care products affordable and accessible to everyone.

To us, health is not the absence of disease, but a state of being confident with how you live, feel, look and perform. All of this translates to SELFCARE. We engage in some form of selfcare daily with food choices, exercise, sleep, skincare and dental care. But practising selfcare can still be hard, expensive, or simply difficult to know where to begin.

That is why we started with skin health. After launching our primary care services, with dermatology among them, we met clients with skin conditions such as acne who wanted treatment from us. It turned out that the treatment we prescribed, only a few could afford leaving them unable to start. Some could manage their first treatment cycle, but couldn't afford to refill.

Affordability was not the only problem. Accessibility was a pain too. Clients waited 3–4 weeks from consultation to treatment, because we had to source products from Europe and the US. Even when the product price was affordable, the shipping cost pushed the total up by 3 to 5 folds and those brands have no formal operations in Africa, so retailers are few and prices stay high.

So we spent months researching ingredients and product formulations, manufacturing and supply chain. It became clear that we could make high quality, global-standard products ourselves and make them affordable and accessible to everyone. The success we had managing and treating various skin conditions became our source of inspiration and information.

"Empowering everyone to be the best version of themselves so they can feel, look, perform and live better."

Our Intention

Protecting the earth
honestly.

VITAL is not a sustainable beauty company, as we don't have all the answers yet. VITAL was founded on transparency, and we believe that any business based on consumption (including ours) cannot be truly and fully sustainable within the current limits of sustainability in business.

We are working hard to ensure that we are minimising our impact, but we don't have all the answers figured out, and we want to be transparent about where we are on this journey.

We believe doing good for people is just not enough anymore. Our survival depends on doing good for both people and the planet. It is time to change what we have been consuming time to question what goes inside, not just in what we eat but also what we apply on our bodies. As much as we are making products that are clean and safe for our health, it is time we examine the impact of these products on the health of life around us.

Our products are powered by active ingredients extracted from plants that thrive on our planet like our antioxidants extracted from Barbados cherry, and green tea from tea plants. This helps us make safe, clean and effective formulas that celebrate and respect what our planet has to offer. We are obliged to take care of this planet.

Initiatives we're considering

  • More responsible packaging
  • Fighting climate change
  • Better waste management
  • Responsible sourcing and product lifecycle
  • Water conservation

We went back to the question: what can we do to make products that consumers don't have to choose between what's effective, what's right for them, and what's good for the planet? Again, we don't have all the answers, but we are actively seeking them. Having no answers shouldn't be an excuse.
